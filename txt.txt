**How much time does your team waste turning meeting notes into actual Jira tasks?** 🤦‍♂️

We've all been there: a 60-minute Zoom session full of great ideas, completely lost in a messy, unstructured text transcript. 

To solve this, I built **Chaos to Sprint** 🚀 – an AI-powered analyzer that transforms chaotic meeting transcripts into structured, execution-ready sprint plans in seconds.

**How I built it:**
🔹 **Backend:** Fast, asynchronous **FastAPI (Python)** integrated with **OpenAI's API** using advanced prompt engineering to ensure structured, reliable data extraction.
🔹 **Frontend:** A responsive, clean UI built with **React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS**.
🔹 **DevOps/Deployment:** Fully decoupled and deployed – Backend on **Render** (with CORS security policies) and Frontend on **Netlify**.

This project, one of many, allowed me to dive deep into LLM integration, asynchronous backend architecture, and seamless full-stack deployment.
